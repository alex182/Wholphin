# Bundled fonts

The accessibility fork bundles these fonts in `app/src/main/res/font/`. All are licensed under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/).

| Font | Files | Copyright | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Atkinson Hyperlegible | `atkinson_hyperlegible_regular.ttf`, `atkinson_hyperlegible_bold.ttf` | Braille Institute of America, Inc. | https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/atkinsonhyperlegible |
| Lexend | `lexend_variable.ttf` | The Lexend Project Authors | https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lexend |
| OpenDyslexic | `opendyslexic_regular.otf`, `opendyslexic_bold.otf` | Abbie Gonzalez | https://github.com/antijingoist/opendyslexic |

Font Awesome (`fa_solid_900.ttf`) comes from upstream Wholphin.
